PicTalk Quick-Start Guide

Take a picture. Say what you see. Your site walk is saved together.

PicTalk keeps each photo, your voice note, and a written copy of your words together as a stop, and groups the stops into a job. It works with no signal: everything saves on your phone first and uploads when signal comes back.

Your first job in five steps

1
Tap Start New JobThe bar at the top shows the job your stops are saving to.
2
Tap Take PhotoTake a picture of the spot you're looking at.
3
Tap the Tap to Talk button and say what you seeTap it again to stop. PicTalk writes your words down for you.
4
Tap Save This StopRepeat steps 2 to 4 at each spot on the site.
5
Tap End Job when the walk is doneAdd the customer and location, then finish. You get an AI summary to review and a PDF report to share.

Tips for great results

  • One stop per spot. Photo first, then talk about that spot.
  • Say where you are first: "Front entrance, east side."
  • Talk like you're briefing a coworker: what you see, what's wrong, what needs to be done.
  • Give numbers: how many, how far, how big.
  • Read model and part numbers slowly, and spell letters that sound alike.
  • Keep notes short: several short stops beat one long one.

Good to know

  • Before you go: put PicTalk on your home screen so it opens with no signal.
  • Quiet spot, phone close: wind and running equipment make words harder to catch.
  • Check your words before you leave the site and fix anything that came out wrong.
  • Use wrap-up notes at the end for the big picture and what the customer asked for.
  • Review the AI summary before you share the report.
  • Save your work (sign in) so your jobs are kept.
